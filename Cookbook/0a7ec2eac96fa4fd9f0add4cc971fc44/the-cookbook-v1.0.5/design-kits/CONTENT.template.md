# CONTENT.md — what your landing page SAYS (and in what order)

*This is the twin of `DESIGN.md`. DESIGN.md decides how the page LOOKS (fork a reference). CONTENT.md decides what it SAYS and in what ORDER — the sections that actually turn a visitor into a booking. You do NOT fill this from a blank: the `STOREFRONT-BRIEF` prompt has Claude DERIVE these sections from your `PRODUCT-BRIEF.md` (hero ← solution+audience, problem ← problem, offer ← offer, why-us ← why-you) and interview only the gaps. This file is the SHAPE Claude writes to, and the yardstick you judge the draft against.*

**Why order matters:** a landing page isn't a brochure — it's a path. It answers the visitor's questions in the order they ask them: *What is this? Is it for me? Can I trust you? What do I do?* Put those out of order and people leave. This section order comes from real conversion frameworks (StoryBrand, and how high-converting pages are actually built) — you don't have to invent it.

**Three rules that beat every fancy trick** (tell Claude these):
1. **The customer is the hero, you are the guide.** The page is about THEIR problem and outcome, not
"chúng tôi có 10 năm kinh nghiệm." Your credentials are proof — they go in the proof section, never the headline.
2. **One page, one action.** Every button says the SAME thing (all "Giữ chỗ"), repeated a few times
down the page. A menu of choices ("đăng ký HOẶC gọi HOẶC tải") kills conversion.
3. **Strongest proof sits next to the button.** A real testimonial right before the CTA converts; the
same testimonial buried at the bottom does nothing.

## The sections (in order) — a REQUIRED CHECKLIST, not a menu

This is the CRO section contract for a spa storefront. The **[core]** sections MUST ALL be present, in this order — a page missing any of them is not a landing page, it's a fragment. The **[if you have it]** ones are written only if you genuinely have the material (never fake a testimonial); if you skip one, that's a real decision, not an oversight. Claude drafts every section from your `PRODUCT-BRIEF.md`; you sharpen what's off — you never start from a blank.

**Count the sections built vs this list; any core section missing = the page is NOT done.** A thin hero + a CTA is the most common slop shape — it looks finished and converts nothing.

Required checklist (tick each against the built page):
- [ ] 1 · HERO **[core]**
- [ ] 2 · THE PROBLEM **[core]**
- [ ] 3 · WHAT WE OFFER **[core]**
- [ ] 4 · WHY US **[core]**
- [ ] 5 · PROOF **[if real]** — skip only if you have no real testimonial
- [ ] 6 · THE ACTION / CTA BAND **[core]**
- [ ] 7 · FAQ / OBJECTIONS **[if useful]**
- [ ] 8 · FINAL CTA **[core]**

| # | Section | What it holds | Fill it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HERO** [core] | The outcome, not the feature. Headline = what the customer gets. Sub-line = who it's for + the promise, one line. ONE button. | Headline: `[…]` · Sub: `[…]` · Button: `Giữ chỗ` |
| 2 | **THE PROBLEM** [core] | The small pain you remove, in the customer's words. (For a spa: the hassle of calling to book, never knowing if a slot's free.) Keep it short and honest. | `[…]` |
| 3 | **WHAT WE OFFER** [core] | Your services + hours, as what the customer gets — not a raw list. The 3 things that matter most. | `[service · service · service]` · Hours: `[…]` |
| 4 | **WHY US** [core] | ONE honest reason to choose you — a real differentiator, not "chuyên nghiệp · tận tâm · uy tín" (that's noise). | `[…]` |
| 5 | **PROOF** [if you have it] | A real customer's words with a specific result, a named face, a real number ("200+ khách mỗi tháng"). Put it right before a button. NO fake testimonials — skip the section rather than invent one. | `[…]` or SKIP |
| 6 | **THE ACTION (CTA band)** [core] | Restate the outcome in one line + the same button. This is the waitlist join for now — "Để lại email, tụi mình báo bạn ngay khi mở đặt lịch." | `[…]` + `Giữ chỗ` |
| 7 | **FAQ / OBJECTIONS** [if useful] | Answer the 2-3 real hesitations ("Đặt xong có chắc có chỗ không?" "Trả tiền thế nào?"). Honesty raises trust. | `[…]` |
| 8 | **FINAL CTA** [core] | One last line of the outcome + the same button again. Someone who scrolled to the bottom is interested — give them the action. | `[…]` + `Giữ chỗ` |

> **Ban list (tell Claude):** no benefit-soup ("nâng tầm trải nghiệm", "đẳng cấp", "hoàn hảo"). No
> operator-as-hero headline. No competing buttons — ONE action, repeated. No feature-dump hero ("5 dịch
> vụ, 8 kỹ thuật viên, mở cửa 7 ngày") — lead with the customer's outcome, save the list for section 3.

## Make it REAL — optional sections that turn a landing into a believable business site

*The 8 core sections above are the CRO spine. But a real business has MORE than a landing — a page
that stops at 8 sections can feel like a demo. Add the ones below that you genuinely have material
for. Each still obeys the craft bar (numbered eyebrow, real composition — never a bare list). Pick
the 3–5 that fit your business; don't add all of them (a page of 15 thin sections is worse than 10
real ones).*

| Section | What it holds | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **THE STORY / ABOUT** | Why this place exists — the founder's real reason, in their voice. The emotional anchor. | homestay, café, brand, personal |
| **A DAY / THE EXPERIENCE** | A timeline or narrative of what it's actually like (6:00 → tối). Sells by letting them imagine being there. | homestay, retreat, tour, spa |
| **AMENITIES / TIỆN NGHI** | The concrete things included — as an editorial grid with icons, not a bullet dump. | homestay, hotel, venue |
| **THE SPACE / GALLERY** | Real photos — a full-bleed mosaic or a pinned horizontal gallery (the signature move). Real photos only. | any visual business |
| **LOCATION / MAP** | Where it is + how to get there — an embedded or styled map, address, "cách trung tâm X phút". | homestay, café, clinic, venue |
| **THE HOST / TEAM** | The real people — a named face + one honest line. Builds trust that a logo can't. | homestay, service, clinic |
| **NEARBY / QUANH ĐÂY** | What's around — things to do, places to eat. Makes it a real place in a real world. | homestay, hotel, tour |
| **RULES / LƯU Ý** | House rules, check-in/out, what to bring. The practical stuff a real guest needs. | homestay, rental, venue |
| **SEASONAL / OFFER NOTE** | A time-specific note ("mùa này sương dày nhất", a real promo). Freshness = a living business. | any |
| **FAQ** | The 3–4 real hesitations answered honestly. (Already in the core list — expand it if useful.) | any |

**The rule:** every added section still needs a numbered eyebrow, real composition, and honest
content. Adding sections makes the site feel REAL only if each one is genuine — a fake "team" or a
stock "gallery" makes it feel MORE like a demo, not less.

## Filled example — a spa (this is what "good" looks like)

*Copy the shape, not the words — your spa is different. But this is a real, converting structure.*

**1. HERO**
> **Đặt lịch spa trong 30 giây — không cần gọi điện.**
> Cho dân văn phòng bận rộn ở Q1: chọn dịch vụ, chọn giờ rảnh của bạn, xong. *(button: Giữ chỗ)*

**2. THE PROBLEM**
> Muốn đi spa mà ngại gọi điện giờ hành chính, gọi xong còn chưa chắc còn chỗ. Đặt tới đặt lui mệt.

**3. WHAT WE OFFER**
> Massage trị liệu · Chăm sóc da mặt · Gội đầu dưỡng sinh. Mở cửa 9h–21h, cả cuối tuần.

**4. WHY US**
> Kỹ thuật viên cố định — bạn chọn được đúng người quen tay, không phải ai rảnh thì làm.

**5. PROOF** *(only if real)*
> "Đặt trên web 20 giây, tới nơi có người chờ sẵn đúng giờ." — Chị Lan, khách quen 2 năm.
> *(if you don't have a real quote yet: SKIP this section — don't fake it. Add it after you open.)*

**6. THE ACTION (waitlist)**
> Tụi mình đang mở đặt lịch online. Để lại email — bạn là người đầu tiên được báo khi chạy. *(Giữ chỗ)*

**7. FAQ**
> *Đặt xong có chắc có chỗ?* Có — chỗ được giữ cho bạn, không ai đặt trùng được.
> *Trả tiền sao?* Đặt cọc giữ chỗ, phần còn lại trả tại tiệm.

**8. FINAL CTA**
> Bớt một cuộc gọi, chắc một chỗ ngồi. Để lại email, tụi mình báo bạn ngay. *(Giữ chỗ)*

## How to CREATE this file (direct Claude — you don't write it)

Same move as DESIGN.md: you make the calls, Claude drafts.

1. Paste to Claude:
   > "Create a CONTENT.md for my spa landing page, using the section order in the template
   > (`assets/CONTENT.template.md`). Draft each section FROM my brief — customer as the hero, ONE
   > button 'Giữ chỗ', no benefit-soup. Leave PROOF as a slot; I'll only add a real testimonial. Show
   > me the draft so I can fix the words."
2. Claude drafts the sections in order. **You read it as the owner:** is the hero about the CUSTOMER's
outcome (not my bio)? Is there exactly ONE button intent? Did any "đẳng cấp / nâng tầm" soup sneak in? Cut it, tell Claude, it edits.
3. Save as `CONTENT.md`.

## How the two files work together

- **CONTENT.md** = what the page says + section order (this file).
- **DESIGN.md** = how it looks (fork a reference).
- Build prompt: **"Build my landing page. Use CONTENT.md for the sections and copy, and DESIGN.md for
the look. Show me your plan first."** Then run `taste-check.md`.
